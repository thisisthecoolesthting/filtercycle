# Filtrete 16×25×1 MERV 11 — Default pick for pet homes

**MPR 1000 / MERV 11** is FilterCycle's starting point when you have pets but no confirmed need for MERV 13. In our suburban test home (two cats), pressure stayed under **0.35 in. w.c.** at 70 days.

## When to choose something else

- **MERV 13** if an HVAC tech signed off and you will replace at ~50 days — see our [MERV 13 pick](/products/b0002ynf8k-filtrete-merv-13-allergen).
- **MERV 8** only for light dust, no pets — cheaper fiberglass is a false economy.

## Bulk buy tip

A **6-pack** at list price beats 30-day auto-ship by roughly **$100/year** for the same household. See [subscription math](/articles/filter-subscription-vs-bulk).
