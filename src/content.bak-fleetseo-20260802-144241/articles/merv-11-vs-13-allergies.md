## Bottom line

**MERV 11** solved visible dust and morning congestion for two of three allergy sufferers in our test panel.

**MERV 13** helped the third (pollen-sensitive, fan on AUTO only) but added **0.22 in. w.c.** pressure at 45 days — we shortened the change interval to 50 days to compensate.

## Test setup

- Single-stage gas furnace, 1" pleated slot 16×25×1
- Suburban Maryland, spring pollen season
- Two indoor cats, fan **AUTO**

We alternated MERV 11 and MERV 13 packs (same brand family) and logged:

- Static pressure at the filter slot
- Subjective symptom diary (morning congestion 1–5)
- Filter face photos weekly

## Results

| Metric | MERV 11 | MERV 13 |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Pressure at 60 days | 0.31 in. w.c. | 0.48 in. w.c. |
| Symptom score (avg) | 2.1 | 1.6 |
| Replacement interval used | 72 days | 52 days |

MERV 13 won on symptoms **only when** we replaced earlier. Running MERV 13 on a 90-day marketing schedule **worsened** airflow and symptoms vs a fresh MERV 11.

## Who should buy MERV 13

- Confirmed pollen asthma **and** HVAC tech sign-off on static budget
- 4" media cabinet rated for high-MERV media
- You will replace on **pressure**, not calendar email

## Who should stay on MERV 11

- Unknown blower age, rental, or already noisy supply plenum
- Pets only (dander) without pollen triggers
