# GE MWF — Part-number fidelity matters

GE labels **6 months / 200 gallons**. FilterCycle light-use homes (two people, municipal water) often run **9 months** with normal flow and taste.

## Replace early if

- Ice cubes smell stale
- Dispenser flow drops by half
- You had a boil-water advisory (swap after event)

## Subscription trap

Quarterly fridge filter ships cost **~$180/year** for one cartridge slot. A **two-pack** on demand is usually **under $90/year** for the same usage.
