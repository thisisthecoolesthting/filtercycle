## The 8 Best Smart Air Purifiers for Connected Living

Smart air purifiers are revolutionizing the way we manage indoor air quality. With features like app control, real-time air quality monitoring, and voice command compatibility, these devices offer convenience and efficiency. Whether you’re dealing with allergies, pet dander, or wildfire smoke, a smart air purifier can help you breathe easier. Here are eight of the best options available today.

### 1. **Dyson Purifier Humidify+Cool Formaldehyde**
Dyson’s Purifier Humidify+Cool Formaldehyde is a multifunctional device that combines air purification, humidification, and cooling. It uses a HEPA filter to capture 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns, including allergens, bacteria, and viruses. What sets it apart is its ability to detect and destroy formaldehyde, a common indoor pollutant. The Dyson Link app lets you monitor air quality, adjust settings, and schedule operations from your smartphone.

### 2. **Blueair Blue Pure 411i Max**
The Blueair Blue Pure 411i Max is a compact yet powerful smart air purifier designed for small to medium-sized rooms. It features a three-stage filtration system, including a HEPA filter and activated carbon, to remove particles, odors, and gases. The device integrates with the Blueair Friend app, allowing you to control it remotely and track air quality in real time. Its minimalist design and quiet operation make it a great addition to any room.

### 3. **Coway Airmega 400S**
The Coway Airmega 400S is a high-capacity smart air purifier ideal for large spaces. It uses a dual filtration system with a Green True HEPA filter and an activated carbon filter to capture pollutants and neutralize odors. The device connects to the Coway IoCare app, which provides detailed air quality data and lets you customize settings. Its auto mode adjusts the fan speed based on air quality, ensuring optimal performance without manual intervention.

### 4. **Levoit Core 400S**
Levoit’s Core 400S offers a sleek design and robust performance at an affordable price. It features a three-stage filtration system with a True HEPA filter, activated carbon filter, and pre-filter to tackle allergens, smoke, and odors. The VeSync app enables remote control, scheduling, and air quality monitoring. The device also supports Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands, making it a versatile choice for smart homes.

### 5. **Honeywell HPA300 Smart**
The Honeywell HPA300 Smart is a reliable air purifier designed for large rooms. It uses a HEPA filter to capture 99.97% of airborne particles, including pollen, dust, and pet dander. The device connects to the Honeywell Home app, which provides real-time air quality updates and allows you to adjust settings remotely. Its turbo mode is particularly effective for quickly cleaning the air in high-pollution scenarios.

### 6. **Winix 5500-2 with Plasmawave**
The Winix 5500-2 is a popular choice for its combination of affordability and advanced features. It includes a True HEPA filter, an activated carbon filter, and Winix’s proprietary Plasmawave technology, which neutralizes pollutants at a molecular level. The device connects to the Winix app for remote control and air quality monitoring. Its auto mode adjusts fan speed based on real-time air quality readings, ensuring efficient operation.

### 7. **Philips Series 3000i Connected**
Philips’ Series 3000i Connected is a premium smart air purifier with a focus on design and performance. It uses a HEPA filter and an activated carbon filter to remove particles, allergens, and odors. The Philips Clean Home+ app provides detailed air quality insights and lets you control the device remotely. Its AeraSense technology continuously monitors air quality and adjusts settings automatically, making it a hassle-free option.

### 8. **Mi Air Purifier 4**
The Mi Air Purifier 4 is a budget-friendly smart air purifier that doesn’t compromise on features. It includes a three-stage filtration system with a HEPA filter to capture particles as small as 0.3 microns. The device connects to the Mi Home app, which offers remote control, scheduling, and air quality monitoring. Its compact design and quiet operation make it ideal for bedrooms or offices.

## What to Look for in a Smart Air Purifier

When choosing a smart air purifier, consider the following factors:

1. **Room Size:** Ensure the purifier is rated for the size of the room where you plan to use it. Larger spaces require higher CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) values.  
2. **Filtration System:** Look for a True HEPA filter for particle removal and an activated carbon filter for odors and gases. Some models offer additional technologies like UV-C light or ionization.  
3. **Smart Features:** Check for app compatibility, real-time air quality monitoring, and voice control integration.  
4. **Noise Level:** If you plan to use the purifier in a bedroom or office, opt for a model with quiet operation.  
5. **Maintenance:** Consider the cost and frequency of filter replacements. Some models feature washable or reusable filters to reduce long-term expenses.  

By selecting a smart air purifier that meets your needs, you can enjoy cleaner air and a healthier living environment with minimal effort.
