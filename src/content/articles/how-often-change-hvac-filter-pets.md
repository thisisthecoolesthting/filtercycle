## Short answer

**One or two indoor pets, MERV 11, average fan runtime:** plan **60–75 days**, not 90.

**Multiple shedding dogs or cats, MERV 13:** **45–60 days** is common in our suburban test home.

## Why pets shorten the cycle

Pet dander and hair fragments are large compared to smoke particles, but they **mat the face** of a pleated filter quickly. That mat raises static pressure long before the media is "chemically expired."

Signs you're overdue:

- Filter gray on the **intake face** within 3 weeks.
- Furnace runs longer to hit thermostat.
- Dust reappears on furniture 24 hours after cleaning.

## MERV choice with pets

- **MERV 11** balances capture and airflow for most 1" systems.
- **MERV 13** only if your installer confirmed blower capacity — otherwise you risk frozen coils in cooling season.

## Grooming and runtime matter as much as MERV

Daily brushing and keeping bedroom doors closed can push a MERV 11 change toward **90 days**. Leaving the fan in **ON** mode 24/7 pulls more lint — expect the **short end** of the range.

## Subscription math

A 30-day auto-ship on MERV 11 16×25×1 filters costs roughly **$180–220/year** for four packs you may not need. Buying a **6-pack** when pressure or the calendar says so usually lands **$90–130/year** for the same home.
