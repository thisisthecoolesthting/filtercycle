## What to Look For in an Air Filter

When selecting replacement air filters, consider both physical dimensions and filtration capabilities. The MERV rating (Minimum Efficiency Reporting Value) indicates how effectively the filter captures particles - higher numbers trap smaller particles but may restrict airflow in some systems. Pleated filters generally offer better filtration than fiberglass, while electrostatic options can attract smaller particles.

## How to Choose the Right Air Filter Size

1. Remove your current filter and check for printed dimensions
2. Measure length, width, and depth with a tape measure (round to nearest 1/4")
3. Note whether your system requires standard or custom sizes
4. Consider your household's air quality needs (pets, allergies, etc.)
5. Check your HVAC manual for any special requirements

Remember that a slightly thicker filter (like replacing 1" with 2") often provides better filtration without modification, while going thinner can cause airflow issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use a smaller air filter if I can't find my exact size?
A: Never use a smaller filter - unfiltered air will bypass the system and potentially damage components.

Q: Why does my 16x25x1 filter measure slightly different?
A: Nominal sizes are rounded for convenience. Always check actual dimensions before purchasing.

Q: How often should I change my air filter?
A: Standard 1-2" filters typically need replacement every 90 days, while thicker filters may last 6-12 months.
